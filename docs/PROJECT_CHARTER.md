# Project charter

## Mission

Investigate intelligence that runs on consumer hardware, improves through experience, compresses
experience into predictive structure/abstractions/causal models/procedures, preserves old skills,
allocates compute adaptively, and keeps a bounded persistent cognitive footprint. Capability should
eventually continue rising while persistent cognitive storage reaches a controlled plateau.

"Better than frontier intelligence" means a different, explicitly multidimensional axis: greater
verified capability gain per parameter, persistent byte, joule, observation, and unit of compute;
efficient continual development; and reusable verified procedures. It does **not** mean more facts,
better chat style, or an uncontrolled aggregate score from a small model. Small models are scientific
instruments that expose architectural effects; an LLM may be one organ, never the whole definition.

## Scope and breakthrough bar

Current scope is laboratory infrastructure and small falsifiable experiments—not a final cognitive
architecture. A breakthrough requires a replicated, held-out, equal-resource improvement that
survives ablation and adversarial testing, identifies the resource that paid for it, and is not
explained by more storage, data, parameters, context, attempts, tool access, or hidden telemetry.

A demonstration, benchmark leak, cherry-picked run, prompt trick, unpriced resource increase, or
gain that disappears under matched resources is not a breakthrough. Negative and disproven results
are useful evidence. Safety, reproducibility, bounded resources, and honest uncertainty outrank a
compelling demo.

## Success trajectory

The long-term success test is a sequence of new tasks where capability improves, transfer rises,
interference stays controlled, raw experience is compressed or discarded, and persistent cognitive
bytes remain within a predeclared plateau budget. The claim remains a project hypothesis until such
evidence exists.
